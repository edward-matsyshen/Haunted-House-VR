# 👻 Haunted-House-VR

**A spine-chilling Virtual Reality Haunted House, designed with wheelchair accessibility at its core.**  
Created, developed, and designed by **Joe Glass**, **Calvin Ho**, and **Edward Matsyshen** in collaboration with **MXTReality** at **Bellevue College**.

## 🕯️ About the Game
Step into a **dark, immersive haunted house**, where every creak of the floorboards and every flicker of candlelight builds suspense.  
This VR experience was crafted to ensure that **players using wheelchairs can fully enjoy and navigate the game**, without sacrificing the thrill of traditional VR horror experiences.

- 🌌 Atmospheric environments that pull you into the unknown.  
- 🦴 Spine-tingling scares and supernatural encounters.  
- 🦽 Thoughtfully designed for wheelchair accessibility.  
- 🎮 Powered by **Oculus VR** technology.
